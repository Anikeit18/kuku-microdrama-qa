Catch broken AI episodes before they become broken promos

A QA gate that runs on each AI-generated microdrama episode and its screenplay, just before promo cutting. It flags black frames, frozen picture, unexpected silence, distorted audio, outfit and background changes inside a scene, and broken lip sync, with timestamps and frames, then gives a pass / review / fail verdict.

The problem

AI pipelines generate each shot separately, then stitch picture, voice and lip sync together. Those seams produce exactly these defects. When one reaches a promo, it burns paid reach at the moment a new viewer decides whether to watch. Today an editor has to watch every episode to catch them.

1 · InputEpisode video + timestamped screenplay
2 · Technicalffmpeg: black, freeze, silence, clipping, A/V start offset
3 · ContinuityShots → background colour (people masked) and clothing under the main face, per scripted scene
4 · Lip syncYuNet finds faces → MediaPipe mouth opening on a face crop ↔ speech loudness, ±0.5 s
5 · VerdictBlocker → FAIL · major → REVIEW · else PASS. Report with frames

The screenplay says what's intended: a scripted fade isn't an error, silence over a scripted line is a blocker, a scene is one place with a fixed cast. An optional vision LLM (Gemini or any OpenAI-compatible model, swapped with an environment variable) can give a second opinion on continuity flags. It can only downgrade a flag.

Where it fails

  • Wrong audio track missed: faces on screen, but the mouth couldn't be measured at those angles.
  • Lip sync compares loudness with mouth opening. It can't tell if the wrong words are mouthed.
  • Intent comes from the script. Unscripted stylistic fades and unlisted locations get flagged.
  • Outfit = colour: a same-colour swap is missed. Clips were 8–12 s; next step is ~20 real episodes labelled by an editor.

How a team would know it works

  • Share of flags the editor marks real (target >60%)
  • Blocker defects later found in published promos (target ≈0)
  • Editor minutes per episode spent on technical review
  • Regenerations moved before promo cutting, not after
